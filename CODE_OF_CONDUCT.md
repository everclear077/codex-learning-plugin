# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Critique ideas, code and teaching choices rather than personal characteristics. Harassment, threats, doxxing and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

Use minimal, redacted examples in reports. A strict project-critique teaching mode is not permission to insult learners or contributors. Maintainers may moderate or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

For sensitive conduct concerns, request a private channel from the maintainers without posting identifying details publicly. Do not promise confidentiality on behalf of services you do not control.
